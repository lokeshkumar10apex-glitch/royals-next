Corporate events in Jaipur bring together people from across the country — and often the world. Choosing the right catering can make or break the experience for your guests.

## Inclusivity Comes First

India has a significant vegetarian population. When you choose pure veg catering for a corporate event, you ensure **100% of your guests** can eat everything on the menu without hesitation. No separate queues, no awkward conversations.

## Hygiene is Non-Negotiable

Pure veg kitchens eliminate cross-contamination concerns that come with mixed menus. At Royal Caterers, our kitchen follows strict hygiene protocols — everything is prepared fresh on the day of the event.

## What a Good Corporate Menu Looks Like

A well-planned corporate catering menu has three parts:

### Welcome Refreshments
- Fresh fruit juices or nimbu pani
- Light snacks — mini samosas, dhokla, or sandwiches

### Main Course
- 2 dry sabzis + 1 gravy
- Dal + rice + assorted breads
- Raita and salad bar

### Dessert
- One warm dessert (gulab jamun or halwa)
- One cold dessert (kulfi or ice cream counter)

## Punctuality Matters in Corporate Settings

Corporate events run on tight schedules. Your caterer must be set up **30 minutes before** your event starts, not during. Always clarify setup time when booking.

## The Cost Advantage

Pure veg catering for corporate events is consistently more cost-effective than mixed menus. The ingredient sourcing is simpler and wastage is easier to manage.
