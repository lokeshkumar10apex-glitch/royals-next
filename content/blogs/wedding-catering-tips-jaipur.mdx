Planning a wedding in Jaipur is an exciting journey, but the catering side of things can feel overwhelming. After catering 5,000+ events across Rajasthan, here is what we have learned.

## 1. Finalise Your Guest Count Early

Catering is priced per plate, so the guest count drives your entire budget. Get a confirmed estimate at least **3 months before** the wedding.

## 2. Choose a Seasonal Menu

Jaipur summers are hot — heavy fried food in peak May heat is not ideal. Opt for lighter starters and refreshing drinks. In winter, hearty dal baati and live tawa counters are crowd favourites.

## 3. Always Include a Live Counter

Live counters — chaat, pav bhaji, or dosa — are the most photographed part of any wedding food setup. They keep guests entertained and the food comes fresh.

## 4. Plan for Dietary Requirements

Even in a pure veg setup, guests may have Jain requirements (no onion/garlic), diabetic restrictions, or nut allergies. Inform your caterer at least 2 weeks in advance.

## 5. Taste Test Before You Confirm

Never confirm a caterer without doing a food tasting. At Royal Caterers, we offer a complimentary tasting session for weddings above a certain size.

## 6. Confirm Serving Staff Ratio

For a comfortable experience, you need at least **1 server per 25 guests**. For a sit-down dinner, aim for 1 per 15 guests.

## 7. Discuss Presentation Early

The way food is presented is as important as the taste. Discuss table layout, chafing dish styles, and decoration with your caterer well in advance.

## 8. Have a Backup Plan for Rain

Outdoor Jaipur weddings in monsoon season need a covered backup. Discuss contingency setup with your caterer and venue together.

## 9. Confirm Waste Management

A good caterer will handle leftover food responsibly — either redistribution to staff or donation. Ask about this before signing.

## 10. Book Early, Especially for Wedding Season

Jaipur wedding season (October–February) fills up fast. The best caterers are booked 6–8 months in advance. Do not wait until the last minute.
